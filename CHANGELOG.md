# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-21) · draft

First public draft of the ontology.

- Six layers: Quran, work, content unit, interpretation, provenance and review, concept (mafhūm)
- Class `mfh:Frage` (question on a passage) and topic areas `mfh:Themenbereich`
- Controlled lists for genre, school, unit type, stance, aspect type and review status
- Labels in Arabic, Turkish, English and German for all 130 terms (71 Arabic labels marked as unreviewed suggestions)
- Extends SemanticTafsir (MIT)
